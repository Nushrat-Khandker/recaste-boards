1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Recaste ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information in connection with the mobile application Noor – Sunset to Sunset Day View (Application ID: com.example.myapplication / com.noor.dayview).

We are dedicated to user privacy and practice privacy-by-design. The application functions predominantly offline, performs all astronomical and prayer calculations locally on your physical device, and does not collect or sell your personal information.

2. Location Data Usage & Protection

The application requests access to location services (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).

Purpose of Location Access:
  • Calculating precise solar and lunar ephemeris (exact sunset, civil/nautical/astronomical twilight, sunrise, and solar noon).
  • Calculating Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha) tailored to your geographic latitude and longitude.
  • Rendering localized moon phases and horizon tilts.
Strict Local Processing Guarantee: Location coordinates are processed exclusively on-device in real time. Your location data is never transmitted to our servers, never sent to external third parties, never logged remotely, and never sold.

3. Local Data Storage & User Content

When you use features such as scheduling tasks, notes, or timeline event blocks:

  • All content is stored strictly within your device's local sandboxed database (Android Room / SQLite).
  • We do not operate remote servers to index, sync, or read your personal calendar blocks, tasks, or notes.
  • You maintain exclusive control and ownership over your personal data.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not share, disclose, or sell personal information to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

The application relies on core Android runtime libraries and Google Play Services for standard app distribution, licensing, and crash stability. No third-party behavioral tracking or advertising SDKs are included.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because all user content is stored locally on your device:

  • Individual Deletion: You can delete individual events or tasks at any time directly through the app interface.
  • Complete Deletion: You can remove all application data at any time by going to your Android device's Settings > Apps > Noor > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is harvested or transmitted, our service fully adheres to COPPA and global child privacy guidelines.

7. Security

We protect your privacy by keeping your data on your device within Android's sandboxed environment. We practice least-privilege permission management and do not store sensitive user credentials or tracking tokens.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published at https://recaste.com/t0/privacy with the revised effective date clearly indicated.
